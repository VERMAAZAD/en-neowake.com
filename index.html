Why It Works

The Mitochondria Effect

Your mitochondria contain light-sensitive proteins called chromophores. When they absorb specific wavelengths of light, they dramatically increase ATP production — the energy molecule that powers every function in your body.

More ATP means more energy for repair, regeneration, and optimal function. This is why PBM users report improvements across such a wide range of conditions — from pain to sleep to skin to energy.

The wrist is uniquely positioned for systemic delivery: the radial artery runs just beneath the surface, so light-activated compounds circulate through your entire body with every heartbeat.

How is this different from a regular smartwatch?+
The Chroma Watch is a therapeutic device, not a fitness tracker. Smartwatches monitor your health. The Chroma Watch actively improves it through photobiomodulation — emitting clinically relevant wavelengths of light and low-level laser energy to stimulate cellular processes, improve circulation, and support your body's natural healing mechanisms.
Is light therapy through the wrist actually effective?+
Yes — the wrist is an ideal location because the radial artery passes very close to the skin surface, allowing laser and light energy to directly irradiate blood as it circulates. This means light-activated compounds travel throughout your entire body with each heartbeat. Over 4,000 peer-reviewed studies support PBM's efficacy.
When will I see results?+
Some users feel improvements in energy and sleep within 1–2 weeks. For deeper benefits — pain reduction, enhanced recovery, improved circulation — most users see meaningful changes at the 4–8 week mark. Results compound over time with consistent daily use.
Is it safe? Are there any contraindications?+
Low-level laser therapy and photobiomodulation have an excellent safety record when used as directed. The device uses low-power, non-thermal light that does not heat tissue. However, individuals with photosensitivity disorders, those on photosensitizing medications, pregnant women, or people with serious medical conditions should consult a physician first.

The Chroma Watch is designed for all-day wear — the therapeutic light works continuously while you go about your normal routine. You can wear it during work, exercise, and relaxation. The more consistently you wear it, the faster and more pronounced the cumulative benefits.
