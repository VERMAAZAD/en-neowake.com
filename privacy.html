Neowake® Chroma Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Neowake® Chroma collects, protects, and uses your information while accessing our wellness technology platform, neuro-acoustic experiences, and wearable frequency products.

At Neowake® Chroma , we are committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining transparency regarding how your information is collected and used. We follow responsible practices to provide a safe, secure, and user-friendly wellness experience.

1. Information We Collect

When visiting the Neowake® Chroma website, we may collect certain information to improve website functionality, customer experience, product support, and order processing.

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, billing details, and shipping information during purchases.
  • Technical information including browser type, IP address, device information, operating system, and on-site activity.
  • Information voluntarily submitted through forms, subscriptions, customer support requests, or wellness newsletters.

2. How We Use Your Information

Neowake® Chroma uses collected information to improve user experience, provide customer support, and optimize wellness product services.

  • To securely process and fulfill customer orders.
  • To provide updates regarding purchases, support requests, shipping, and wellness-related announcements.
  • To improve website performance, user experience, and product functionality.
  • To deliver educational content, promotions, and wellness updates when users voluntarily subscribe.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to personalize browsing experiences, analyze traffic, and improve website speed and usability.

Cookies help us understand visitor interactions and optimize website performance for better navigation and faster access to important content.

Users may disable cookies through browser settings if preferred, although some website features may not function properly afterward.

4. Payment & Transaction Security

Protecting financial information is extremely important to us. Neowake® Chroma uses secure checkout technologies and encrypted payment systems to safeguard customer transactions.

Sensitive payment information is processed through trusted third-party payment providers using industry-standard security practices.

5. Third-Party Services

We may work with trusted third-party providers for analytics, marketing, payment processing, customer support, and shipping services.

These providers only receive information necessary to perform their services and are expected to maintain appropriate confidentiality and security standards.

Neowake® Chroma is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites linked from our pages.

6. Email Communication

Users who subscribe to our newsletter may receive updates about wellness technology, meditation support content, new product launches, promotions, and educational frequency-related resources.

Users may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link provided in emails.

7. Data Protection & Security

Neowake® Chroma follows modern security practices to help protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

While we implement reasonable security safeguards, no online platform or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Privacy Matters

We believe every user deserves transparency, security, and control over personal information. Neowake® Chroma is committed to maintaining a trustworthy and secure wellness platform for all visitors and customers.

Neowake® Chroma products and services are intended for adults only. Our website is not directed toward children under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Neowake® Chroma support team through the official website.
